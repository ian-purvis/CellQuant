# Code of Conduct

CellQuant follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be respectful, be welcoming to people at every level of experience, give and accept feedback kindly, and keep discussion about the work.

Harassment, insults, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable here, in issues, pull requests, or any other CellQuant space.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/ian-purvis/CellQuant/issues/new) that says what happened without naming anyone you do not want to name; the maintainer ([@ian-purvis](https://github.com/ian-purvis)) will respond. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.
